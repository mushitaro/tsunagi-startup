MOYAI, including the skill packs and the public ledger for waste incineration and night-soil treatment, is open source and free for anyone to use. Municipalities and operators can start trials and real operation on site tomorrow, with no budget allocation and no prior permission. (When presenting or sharing your work, we ask that you credit it as Powered by MOYAI.)
